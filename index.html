HERO IN EXILE

Exiled Prince. Lost Kingdom. One Last Fight.

Level 1: Defeat 5 Enemies | Level 2: Defeat 7 Enemies | Level 3: Defeat 10 Enemies To Win

Controls: Left Right to Move | Space to Attack | Up to Jump | Avoid Enemy Attack To Save HP
Defeat 5 Enemies To Clear Level 1

🎮 HOW TO PLAY

  • Left / Right - Move Hero
  • Space - Sword Attack
  • Up - Jump To Dodge

📜 STORY

Prince Arjun was exiled from his kingdom. Enemies are guarding the kingdom. Defeat all enemies in 3 levels to reclaim the throne.

⚕️ HP RULES

  • Enemy Touch = HP Decreases
  • Enemy Passes You = HP -10
  • Enemy Hits You = HP -15
  • Level Clear = HP +20

🏆 WIN / LOSE

  • WIN: Clear All 3 Levels
  • LOSE: HP Becomes 0
